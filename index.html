What I run

RPL Fast Track

Melton West, Victoria

Helps experienced workers in Australia get their skills formally recognised through Recognition of Prior Learning.

We guide people in aged care, disability support, early childhood, community services and mental health through eligibility, evidence and the application. Assessment and certification are done by our partner Registered Training Organisation — we make the path clear and the paperwork right.

Visit rplfastrack.com

EduGen Global

Mirpur, Dhaka

Guides Bangladeshi students to undergraduate and postgraduate study abroad.

From choosing a course and country — the UK, Malaysia, Korea and more — to documents, applications and visa preparation, we keep students and families informed at every step.

Visit edugenglobal.com

How I work

Honest promises only

About me

I'm Tanvir, an entrepreneur working across Bangladesh and Australia in education and skills recognition.

I started in this field because I kept meeting capable people held back by paperwork rather than ability — workers with years of experience but no certificate, and students with strong results but no clear route abroad. My businesses exist to close that gap.

Day to day I lead hiring and training, oversee marketing in two languages, and build the digital tools our teams rely on.
